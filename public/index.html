F-16 Fighting Falcon

TEAM ISLAND WARFARE: Blue and Red teams spawn at separate island airbases, each with its own runway and city. Matches last 10 minutes: destroy the enemy city (bombs, gun, missiles) and shoot down enemy jets (building = 10 pts, jet kill = 100 pts). Blue arrows/boxes track friends, red track enemies. Take off, find enemy fighters over the sea, lock them and engage with missiles or the 20 mm cannon. Weapons, aircraft damage, kills and respawns are synchronized through the multiplayer server. Use the Multiplayer panel to create or join a room.

Left pad pitch and roll (drag down to pull up). Right slider throttle, top zone is afterburner. Hold the nose-up stick past about 85 m/s (165 kt) to rotate, then raise the gear. A target inside the green cone locks after one second; then fire a missile. The gun needs no lock. Bomb drops free-fall bombs: put the yellow diamond on the target. Enemy SAMs lock on for a few seconds before firing (watch for SAM LOCK). Dodge by turning so the missile sits 90° off your nose (beaming) and pressing Flares, or fly below 100 m over the sea.
Keyboard: arrows or WASD, Shift/Ctrl throttle, Space missile, F gun, N bomb, V flares, C view, G gear, B brake, Q/E rudder.
